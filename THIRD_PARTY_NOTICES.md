## HandoverOptimDRL (kit-cel)

`src/handover_bench/baselines/a3.py` adapts the A3 measurement-event logic;
`src/handover_bench/simulator/engine.py` restructures the general
counter/timer state-machine ideas (TTT, T310, N310, N311, HO
preparation/execution, RLF recovery, ping-pong monitoring) from:

> Communications Engineering Lab - KIT, "HandoverOptimDRL",
> https://github.com/kit-cel/HandoverOptimDRL
> Associated paper: J. Voigt, P. J. Gu and P. M. Rost, "A Deep Reinforcement
> Learning-Based Approach for Adaptive Handover Protocols," SCC 2025,
> pp. 1-6, doi: 10.1109/IEEECONF62907.2025.10949111.
